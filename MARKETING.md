# FieldSeal Marketing

## Positioning
**Get paid for the work you did — and prove it.**

FieldSeal is the missing field logger in the Operator Pages stack. Techs capture the job once. The pack is sealed. Clients verify. Disputes die. Invoices get chased with Chaser.

## Target buyers
- Solo techs and 1–5 truck shops (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, general trades)
- Operators already using (or evaluating) VeriQuote / JobSeal / QuantumGuard
- Anyone tired of SaaS field-service platforms that cost $50–$250+/mo and still leave evidence gaps

## Core claims (keep honest)
- Offline after key setup
- Deterministic QuantumGuard CONTRACT-v1 packs
- Clients verify with free open-source QuantumGuard
- Not a full FSM, not PQC, not a payment processor

## Outreach hooks
- “Stop losing money to ‘I didn’t authorize that’ claims.”
- “Seal the job in the truck. Verify anywhere. No monthly fee.”
- Bundle with VeriQuote + Chaser for a complete quote → seal → invoice → chase workflow.
